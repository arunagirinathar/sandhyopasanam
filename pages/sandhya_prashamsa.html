सन्ध्योपासनमयूखः sandhyopāsana-mayūkhaḥ

सन्ध्याप्रशंसा sandhyā-praśaṁsā
Glory of Sandhya

Below are some pramāṇa-vākyās that extol the glory of regularly performing Sandhyāvandanaṁ.

ऋषयो नित्यसन्ध्यत्वाद् दीर्घमायुरवाप्नुवन् । …

ṛṣayo nityasandhyatvād dīrghamāyuravāpnuvan . …

The sages of yore attained long (healthy) life through the regular observance of Sandhyā.

श्रीमन्महाभारतम् अनुशासन पर्वं १०४.१८
śrīmanmahābhāratam anuśāsana parvaṃ 104.18
