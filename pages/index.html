सन्ध्योपासनमयूखः sandhyopāsana-mayūkhaḥ

Sandhyopāsana Mayūkha is a humble digital aid for those wishing to understand and perform the daily practice of Sandhyopāsanam — the time-honored discipline of twilight worship preserved in the Vedic tradition. This website presents a clean, focused interface for accessing accurate timings, Sanskrit prayers, and interpretive notes rooted in traditional sources. Wherever possible, original texts are accompanied by transliteration and simple explanations to guide sincere practitioners.

The intent here is not to innovate or reinterpret, but to support existing practice with clarity and care, drawing light from trusted commentaries and śāstric guidelines. This site is offered in the spirit of service (sevā) and devotion (bhakti).

Currently, Smṛti-Muktāphalaṁ and Dharmasindhuḥ are used as the primary śāstric authorities (pramāṇam). At present, the materials and procedures reflect only the Āpastamba, Drāviḍa tradition of practice (prayoga).
